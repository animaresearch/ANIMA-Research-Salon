# Public Disclosure Scope

This repository contains a deliberately limited portfolio view of ANIMA Research Salon.

## Included

- high-level research questions;
- public milestone summaries;
- aggregate generation outcomes;
- conservative statements of limitations;
- non-confidential descriptions of the research workflow.

## Excluded

- private memory databases and indexes;
- API keys, credentials, account data, and local paths;
- raw model transcripts or hidden reasoning traces;
- private prompts and internal orchestration code;
- unpublished patent-sensitive mechanisms;
- internal claim IDs, archive record IDs, hashes, and provenance maps;
- unreviewed mathematical formulas and detailed proof drafts;
- private evaluation data and model-provider diagnostics.

The absence of an open-source license is intentional. Default copyright applies. This public portfolio does not grant permission to reproduce, modify, distribute, sublicense, or commercialize non-public ANIMA technology.
